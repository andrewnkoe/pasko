Best Time to Visit

Lesotho is a year-round destination — here's what to expect each season.

Spring (Sep–Nov)

Wildflowers bloom across the highlands. Perfect for hiking and horse riding.

Summer (Dec–Feb)

Warm and green. Best for waterfalls and cultural festivals. Some afternoon thunderstorms.

Autumn (Mar–May)

Golden landscapes and clear skies. Ideal for photography and photography tours.

Winter (Jun–Aug)

Snow on the peaks — skiing at AfriSki! Cold but crystal-clear skies for stunning views.
