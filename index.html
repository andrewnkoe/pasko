Why Visit the Kingdom in the Sky?

  • One of only three countries entirely within another country
  • 100% of territory sits above 1,000 m — the world's highest low point
  • Africa's only ski resort at AfriSki (3,222 m)
  • Home to Maletsunyane Falls — one of Africa's highest single-drop waterfalls
  • Ride the legendary Basotho pony through mountain villages
