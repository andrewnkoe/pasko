About Lesotho

Learn about the land, the people and the culture of the Mountain Kingdom.

The Mountain Kingdom

Lesotho is a small, landlocked country entirely surrounded by South Africa. It is one of the few countries in the world to sit entirely above 1,000 metres elevation, earning it the nickname "Kingdom in the Sky".

Lesotho is famous for its breathtaking mountains, the colourful Basotho culture, roaring waterfalls and warm, welcoming people.

Quick Facts

  • Capital city: Maseru
  • Languages: Sesotho and English
  • Currency: Lesotho Loti (LSL)
  • Nickname: The Mountain Kingdom / Kingdom in the Sky
  • Traditional clothing: Basotho blankets & mokorotlo hats
  • Highest point: Thabana Ntlenyana – 3,482 m
  • Population: approximately 2.3 million people
  • Independence: 4 October 1966

Our History

A Kingdom Through Time

Lesotho's rich history spans centuries of resilience, culture and pride.

~1820s

Founding of the Kingdom

King Moshoeshoe I united the Basotho people during the difaqane (forced migrations), establishing the Basotho nation on the fortress mountain of Thaba-Bosiu.

1868

British Protectorate

Lesotho (then Basutoland) became a British protectorate, allowing it to avoid full annexation by South Africa and preserve its sovereignty.

1966

Independence

Lesotho gained full independence from Britain on 4 October 1966, becoming a constitutional monarchy under King Moshoeshoe II.

1986–2000

Lesotho Highlands Water Project

Construction began on Africa's largest water transfer scheme, transforming the economy and bringing Lesotho's mountain rivers to South Africa.

Today

A Rising Destination

Lesotho is growing as an ecotourism and adventure travel gem, welcoming visitors from around the world to its unspoilt highlands and vibrant culture.

Culture & Traditions

Living Culture of the Basotho

The Basotho people maintain proud traditions that colour everyday life across the kingdom.

Basotho Blanket

The iconic Basotho blanket is worn as a symbol of identity, warmth and social status — different patterns mark different life stages.

Famo Music

Famo is a lively traditional music genre, performed with the accordion, drums and vocals — the soundtrack of Lesotho's highlands.

Basotho Pony

These hardy mountain ponies are still the primary form of transport in remote highland villages, and pony trekking is a must-do experience.
