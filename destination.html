Tourist Destinations

Discover thunderous waterfalls, dizzying mountain passes, and sacred alpine nature reserves.

Waterfall 4.9

Maletsunyane Falls

One of Africa's highest single-drop waterfalls, plunging 192 meters into a sheer basalt canyon. Famous for abseiling and misty rainbow views.

Semonkong Alt: 2,200m
Book Tour
Mountain Pass 5.0

Sani Pass Mountain Road

The legendary 4x4 mountain pass scaling the Drakensberg escarpment up to 2,876m, home to the iconic 'Highest Pub in Africa'.

Mokhotlong Border Alt: 2,876m
Book Tour
UNESCO Heritage 4.8

Sehlabathebe National Park

A remote, mystical wilderness of bizarre sandstone monoliths, wildflower meadows, rock pools, and San rock paintings.

Qacha's Nek Alt: 2,400m
Book Tour
Dam & Lake 4.7

Katse Dam Reservoir

At 185 meters high, this engineering marvel forms a sapphire lake amidst towering peaks. Features guided dam wall interior tours and boat cruises.

Thaba-Tseka Alt: 2,050m
Book Tour
Highest Peak 4.9

Thabana Ntlenyana Peak

The "Beautiful Little Mountain" is the highest peak in Southern Africa at 3,482m. A premier trekking destination with views across mountain ranges.

Mokhotlong Alt: 3,482m
Book Tour
Canyon Gorge 4.9

Maletsunyane Canyon & Gorge

The winding green canyon formed over millions of years by the Maletsunyane river. A haven for pony trekking, trout fishing, and wilderness trails.

Semonkong Alt: 2,150m
Book Tour
Culture 4.6

Basotho Hat Craft Centre

Maseru's architectural icon designed in the exact conical shape of a Mokorotlo hat. Browse authentic woven baskets, tapestries, and clay pottery.

Maseru City Crafts & Gifts
Book Tour
Scenic Lookout 4.8

Senqu (Orange) River Canyon

The headwaters of South Africa's longest river meanders majestically through ancient volcanic plateaus, forming awe-inspiring horseshoe canyons.

Southern Highlands Alt: 2,300m
